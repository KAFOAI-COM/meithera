# Meithera — Central Bank Innovation Hub Meeting Brief
**Date:** 27 October 2026. **Purpose:** Regulatory orientation, not authorisation or endorsement.

## Proposed initial activity
Technology-enabled crowdfunding for secured loans to eligible Irish vacant-property refurbishment project owners, potentially through an appropriately authorised ECSP. Meithera would not receive client funds.

## Four unresolved classifications
1. Whether the Stage 1 loan participation and borrower/project owner fit ECSPR.
2. Whether Stage 2 SPV shares/notes are transferable securities under MiFID II and eligible for ECSPR.
3. Whether the Stage 2 per-home structure constitutes an AIF under AIFMD. One SPV per property does **not** by itself resolve this.
4. Whether Meithera can provide technology under an authorised partner and precisely which regulated activities remain with that partner.

## Additional questions
- Who holds security, services defaults, controls client money and handles complaints?
- What legally authoritative register is required, and can Raya Chain be a reconciled audit mirror?
- Which unit or process should receive a legal classification memorandum after this meeting?

## Seven-minute demo
1 minute: problem and scope; 2 minutes: fictional project and investor onboarding; 1 minute: risk warnings and KIIS; 1 minute: simulated transaction; 1 minute: Raya Chain audit mirror; 1 minute: supervisory reporting and questions.

## Non-negotiable boundaries
Demonstration only; no live payments, investment offers, regulatory approval, guaranteed returns or blockchain title. All people, properties and financial figures are illustrative.

## Desired outcome
A decision tree, named follow-up channels and required documentation; not a request for approval in the meeting.
