# Commercial validation and financial modelling specification

## Demand validation (not yet completed)
Interview targets: 20 investors, 10 refurbishment/property operators, 3 authorised partners. Record recruitment source, willingness to invest, desired minimum, risk tolerance, liquidity needs and credible purchase/financing commitments; distinguish intention from actual transaction.

## Lending pilot model
Illustrative principal €160,000; nominal annual rate 7.5%; term 18 months; interest-only payments with principal due at maturity. Annual interest €12,000; monthly €1,000; 18-month total €18,000 **if paid in full and on schedule**, excluding fees, taxes and defaults. Test 0/6/12-month enforcement delays, 10/20/30% recovery shortfalls and refinancing failure. Verify valuation and LTV definition.

## Platform unit economics
Test tickets €100, €500 and €1,000. Model borrower fee (1.5% illustrative) and any ongoing fees against onboarding/KYC, AML, payment processing, transaction/legal, security trustee, servicing, cloud, support, customer acquisition, defaults and partner revenue share. A 1.5% fee on a €100 ticket equals €1.50 gross before costs; do not imply viability.

## Rental SPV model
Include acquisition, stamp duty, legal, survey, refurbishment, contingency, insurance, maintenance, property management, vacancy, arrears, tax at SPV and investor levels, administration, exit and disposal costs. Show yield on total all-in capital, annual cash yield, total return and stressed exit proceeds separately.

## Impact
Count only independently evidenced vacant properties brought back into use, verified BER uplift and completed occupancy. Do not equate property financing with proven homelessness reduction.

## Evidence register
Mark each figure as primary-source verified / third-party claim / internal assumption / unknown; attach publication date and source link.
