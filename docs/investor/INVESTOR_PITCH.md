# MEITHERA — Investor Pitch | October 2026
**Status:** Investor discussion draft. Demonstration only. No live investments, authorisation, signed partners, or guaranteed returns.

## 1. The opportunity
Ireland faces housing supply and refurbishment challenges. Meithera proposes a technology-enabled route for smaller investors to finance eligible vacant-home refurbishment projects.

## 2. The solution
An investor-facing marketplace, regulated-partner-led transaction flow, clear disclosures, and a verifiable audit record. Starting investment €100 is a **product hypothesis**, not a validated commercially sustainable minimum.

## 3. The first wedge
Stage 1: secured lending to vetted refurbishment project owners, potentially via an authorised EU crowdfunding service provider (ECSP), subject to legal classification. Illustrative deal: €160,000, 7.5% per annum, 18 months, with security trustee and independently verified property valuation. **Not an offer.**

## 4. Customer journey
Browse project → understand risks and terms → eligibility / onboarding with responsible authorised entity → KIIS and warnings where applicable → commitment through regulated payments → servicing and reporting. All currently simulated.

## 5. Expansion
Stage 2: per-property SPV economic interests / profit-participating notes and net rental distributions **only if regulatory classification and economics permit**. Stage 3: tenant-to-owner pathways requiring separate legal and affordability assessment. Stage 4: advanced DLT settlement subject to applicable permissions.

## 6. Differentiation
Ireland-focused supply creation; property-by-property disclosure; clear gross-to-net returns; realised-versus-projected outcomes; supplementary Raya Chain audit mirror; potential supervisory reporting.

## 7. Business model (unvalidated)
Borrower arrangement fee (illustrative 1.5% on lending); potential investment administration fees on later products. Model transaction-level servicing, payments, KYC, legal, acquisition, defaults and support costs before committing to pricing.

## 8. Go-to-market
Begin with interviews and partner discovery: target 20 prospective investors, 10 property owners/refurbishment operators and 3 regulated providers. These are **targets, not completed interviews**.

## 9. Economics and risk
Do not present illustrative yields as guaranteed. Include vacancy, refurbishment overrun, borrower default, enforcement delay, refinancing risk, property price change, tax and illiquidity. Show projected versus realised results.

## 10. Technology
Next.js demonstration; simulated onboarding, investor dashboard, compliance controls and Raya Chain explorer. No live funds or actual token transfers. Official off-chain records remain authoritative unless law and authorised infrastructure permit otherwise.

## 11. Roadmap
October: Innovation Hub meeting. November: legal classification and partner discovery. December: investor/property validation and unit economics. Q1 2027: feasibility of first property and technical assurance. Any pilot is conditional on required approvals and partners.

## 12. Partnership / funding discussion
Seeking discussions with regulated crowdfunding providers, property sourcing/refurbishment partners, payment providers, legal counsel and potential pre-seed supporters. **No fundraising amount, valuation, commitments or traction asserted.** Define funding ask only after budget and milestone validation.

**Risk warning:** Property investments involve risk, including possible loss of capital. Returns are not guaranteed and investments may be illiquid.
