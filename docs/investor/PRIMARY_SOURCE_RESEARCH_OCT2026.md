## Verified Irish housing indicators
- CSO Census 2022 final results (Profile 2, April 2022 — still the latest census): **163,433 vacant dwellings** (preliminary was 166,752, of which 48,387 were vacant in both 2016 and 2022). These are not an addressable-market estimate. https://www.cso.ie/en/releasesandpublications/ep/p-cpp2/censusofpopulation2022profile2-housinginireland/keyfindings
- CSO metered-electricity long-term vacancy (experimental, narrower definition): **70,149 (3.2%)**, Q4 2024, published Mar 2026. https://www.cso.ie/en/releasesandpublications/fp/fp-rvmec/residentialvacancybasedonmeteredelectricityconsumption2024/keyfindings
- RTB Rent Index Q1 2026 (latest): new tenancies **€1,839/month, +9.1%** year on year; existing tenancies €1,513, +4.2%. https://esri.ie/publications/the-rtb-rent-index-q1-2026
- CSO RPPI, 12 months to Aug 2026: national median **€400,000**, prices **+5.1%**. https://www.cso.ie/en/releasesandpublications/ep/p-rppi/residentialpropertypriceindexaugust2026/
- CSO 2025 completions: **36,284** dwellings, up **20.4%** on 2024 (as first published, Jan 2026; since revised to 36,215 for 2025 and 30,124 for 2024). H1 2026: **16,679** (Q1 7,856 + Q2 8,823; Q3 not yet published, https://www.cso.ie/en/releasesandpublications/ep/p-ndc/newdwellingcompletionsq22026/). This is housing-market context, not a demand forecast for Meithera. https://www.cso.ie/en/releasesandpublications/ep/p-ndc/newdwellingcompletionsq42025/
- Vacant Property Refurbishment Grant (unchanged in 2026; extended to 2030; homes built before 2008 and vacant 2+ years): up to **€50,000**, with a **€20,000** derelict top-up in qualifying cases. **Registered companies cannot apply**; named owners must qualify. Do NOT include grant income in SPV or corporate refurbishment assumptions without specific legal eligibility confirmation. https://www.gov.ie/en/department-of-housing-local-government-and-heritage/services/vacant-property-refurbishment-grant/

## Regulatory evidence
- Central Bank: ECSPR crowdfunding services concern matching business funding interests of investors and project owners, including facilitating loans and specified securities transactions. Only authorised crowdfunding service providers may perform the regulated service. **Whether Meithera's specific borrower/project and instruments qualify remains an open legal question.** https://www.centralbank.ie/regulation/industry-market-sectors/crowdfunding-service-providers
- CBI explicitly recommends legal advice if unclear on authorisation. https://www.centralbank.ie/regulation/industry-market-sectors/crowdfunding-service-providers/authorisation-process
- AIFs broadly involve raising capital from multiple investors for investment under a defined policy. One-property-per-SPV does not alone eliminate AIF classification. https://www.centralbank.ie/regulation/industry-market-sectors/funds/aifs
