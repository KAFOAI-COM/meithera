# Meithera | Primary-source market and regulatory evidence | 20 October 2026

## Verified Irish housing indicators
- CSO Census 2022 preliminary housing: **166,752 vacant dwellings**, of which **48,387** were vacant in both 2016 and 2022. These are not an addressable-market estimate. https://www.cso.ie/en/releasesandpublications/ep/p-cpr/censusofpopulation2022-preliminaryresults/housing/
- CSO 2025 completions: **36,284** dwellings, up **20.4%** on 2024. This is housing-market context, not a demand forecast for Meithera. https://www.cso.ie/en/releasesandpublications/ep/p-ndc/newdwellingcompletionsq42025/
- Vacant Property Refurbishment Grant: up to **€50,000**, with a **€20,000** derelict top-up in qualifying cases. **Registered companies cannot apply**; named owners must qualify. Do NOT include grant income in SPV or corporate refurbishment assumptions without specific legal eligibility confirmation. https://www.gov.ie/en/department-of-housing-local-government-and-heritage/services/vacant-property-refurbishment-grant/

## Regulatory evidence
- Central Bank: ECSPR crowdfunding services concern matching business funding interests of investors and project owners, including facilitating loans and specified securities transactions. Only authorised crowdfunding service providers may perform the regulated service. **Whether Meithera's specific borrower/project and instruments qualify remains an open legal question.** https://www.centralbank.ie/regulation/industry-market-sectors/crowdfunding-service-providers
- CBI explicitly recommends legal advice if unclear on authorisation. https://www.centralbank.ie/regulation/industry-market-sectors/crowdfunding-service-providers/authorisation-process
- AIFs broadly involve raising capital from multiple investors for investment under a defined policy. One-property-per-SPV does not alone eliminate AIF classification. https://www.centralbank.ie/regulation/industry-market-sectors/funds/aifs
