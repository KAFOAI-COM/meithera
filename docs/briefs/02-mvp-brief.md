# Brief 2 — Meithera MVP (received 2026-10-17)

PROJECT: MEITHERA · MARKET: IRELAND · PRODUCT: Fractional real estate investment platform

OBJECTIVE: Build a professional, investor-ready MVP for an Irish fractional property investment platform, to be demonstrated to the Central Bank of Ireland Innovation Hub.

BUSINESS MODEL: Investors participate through a proposed SPV-based ownership structure. Investors can discover properties, review financials, simulate fractional investments, track projected rental distributions, view portfolio performance, and understand risks and structures.

TECHNOLOGY: Next.js, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, shadcn/ui, PostgreSQL **with PostGIS** (follow-up instruction), Prisma, Authentication, Recharts, responsive design.

DESIGN: Premium Irish fintech aesthetic. Modern, professional, trustworthy. Inspired by institutional investment platforms.

IMPLEMENT: 1 Marketing website · 2 Marketplace · 3 Property details · 4 Return calculator · 5 Investor dashboard · 6 Simulated investment workflow · 7 Investor onboarding · 8 Admin dashboard · 9 Compliance demonstration · 10 Regulatory information section

REGULATORY REQUIREMENTS: label all investments as demonstrations; no real transactions; no claim of Central Bank authorisation; explain the SPV structure; risk warnings; identify regulatory assumptions requiring legal review; never present projected returns as guaranteed.

DEMO DATA: fictional Dublin, Cork, Galway, Limerick properties with valuation, rental income, maintenance expenses, management fees, estimated net yield, investment participation %.

DELIVERABLES: 1 Working MVP · 2 Database schema · 3 Seed data · 4 README.md · 5 Architecture docs · 6 Regulatory assumptions doc · 7 Central Bank demo walkthrough · 8 Investor pitch deck content

WORKFLOW: inspect → plan → build in phases → test & fix → document local run. Start with PROJECT_PLAN.md, ARCHITECTURE.md, REGULATORY_ASSUMPTIONS.md.
