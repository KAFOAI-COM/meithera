# Sprint — Regulatory Validation & Investor Readiness (20–27 October 2026)

**Goal:** Prepare Meithera for the Central Bank Innovation Hub meeting on 27 October and create an investor-ready narrative without implying authorisation or commercial traction.

## P0 — before the meeting
- [ ] Reframe main story: vacant-home refurbishment financing first; fractional rental ownership later.
- [ ] Update landing page, presentation and regulatory demo to distinguish Stage 1 secured lending from Stage 2 SPV notes.
- [ ] Add explicit disclaimer to every pitch: demonstration only; no real funds, investments, authorisation or guaranteed returns.
- [ ] Build an ECSPR / MiFID II / AIFMD decision tree, including partner-led route and regulated-activity ownership.
- [ ] Make four classification questions prominent; do not imply one-SPV-per-home avoids AIF classification.
- [ ] Add client-money, security-trustee, borrower-default and investor-wind-down diagrams.
- [ ] Validate the Stage 1 worked example: €160k loan, 8% annual, 18 months, repayment/refinance and default scenarios.
- [ ] Correct investment return UX: separate projected yield, fees, company tax, investor tax, vacancies, capital loss and liquidity.
- [ ] Create a clear regulator demo click-path with a 7-minute run-through and fallback screenshots.
- [ ] Update competition and evidence tables; flag unverified third-party claims.

## P1 — investor pitch and commercial validation
- [ ] Create a separate investor deck: problem, market, wedge, model, differentiation, GTM, economics, risks, milestones and ask.
- [ ] Avoid invented TAM, traction, signed partners, commitments or fundraising amount.
- [ ] Add interview tracker: 20 prospective investors, 10 property owners/refurbishment operators, 3 authorised providers (targets, not completed).
- [ ] Model unit economics for €100/€500/€1,000 tickets; include KYC, payments, servicing, legal and customer acquisition.
- [ ] Model property-level acquisition, stamp duty, works, contingency, insurance, voids, repairs, property tax and disposal.
- [ ] Compare Stage 1 lending and Stage 2 rental SPV economics and regulatory complexity.
- [ ] Create a measurable impact dashboard: homes returned to use, BER improvement, time to occupation, realised vs projected returns.

## P2 — post-meeting execution
- [ ] Record regulator feedback as questions and follow-ups, not approvals.
- [ ] Commission Irish legal opinion on instruments, ECSPR eligibility, AIFMD, MiFID, AML, tax and promotions.
- [ ] Shortlist authorised ECSP, payment, security trustee and servicing partners.
- [ ] Select one genuine property opportunity for feasibility only, not fundraising.
- [ ] Complete data protection, cybersecurity, operational resilience and continuity assessment.
- [ ] Keep Raya Chain as reconciled audit mirror initially; require legal review before proposing it as official register.
- [ ] Add automated financial-model scenario tests and presentation disclaimer tests.

## Acceptance criteria
1. A regulator can identify proposed regulated activity and accountable authorised party in under 60 seconds.
2. No screen claims Central Bank approval, investor capital protection or guaranteed returns.
3. Every financial return displays assumptions, gross-to-net breakdown and downside case.
4. The investor pitch separates evidence, hypotheses, and roadmap.
5. Live demo operates offline with screenshots as fallback.
6. All unresolved regulatory decisions have owners and next actions.

## Proposed ownership
Founder/product: story, partner outreach and interviews. Legal counsel: classification and documents. Engineering: controls, demo and calculations. Finance/property specialist: underwriting and economics.
