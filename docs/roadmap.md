# Roadmap — the five stages

Source of truth for the on-screen roadmap is `src/lib/roadmap.ts` and `src/lib/stages.ts`; this document mirrors it for reading offline. Timing is indicative and depends on partner agreements and regulatory engagement. The Innovation Hub provides guidance, not approval.

## Badge legend

- **Possible under ECSPR** (mint) — Stages 1 and 2
- **Requires further authorisation** (gold) — Stage 3
- **Future — depends on EU reform** (neutral) — Stage 4
- **Demonstration** — Stage 0 (today)

## Stage 0 · Demo (now — Oct/Nov 2026)

- **Goal:** show the full vision and the staged path; agree the right regulatory route.
- **Have:** Raya Software Limited (CRO); Innovation Hub engagement; this MVP, offline-capable; the Raya Chain concept and simulator.
- **Need:** feedback on the Stage 1 route (own ECSP licence vs licensed partner); a view on the Stage 2 instrument (notes vs shares; fund-rules risk); Irish regulatory counsel.
- **Risks:** misreading what each stage requires; over-promising Stages 3–4.

## Stage 1 · Lending (first loan 2027 H1, subject to partner and regulator)

- **Goal:** fund the refurbishment of vacant homes with secured loans from €100.
- **Product:** first legal charge via a security trustee; fixed interest over 12–24 months paid monthly; borrower pays the 1.5% arrangement fee; LTV ≤ 65%; Raya Chain records lenders, repayments and the charge.
- **Legal basis:** ECSPR lending-based crowdfunding — €5m per project owner in 12 months; KIIS; knowledge test; €1,000 / 5% warning; 4-day reflection period.
- **Partner:** a licensed ECSP such as Property Bridges first; own authorisation later; a payment institution for client money.
- **Need:** ECSP partner agreement or own authorisation; client-money arrangement; the first vacant home and a borrower (project owner); a property manager and a security trustee; a business-continuity plan.
- **Risks:** borrower default and security shortfall; partner dependency and margin; refurbishment overruns delaying repayment.

## Stage 2 · Rent share (2027 H2 / 2028)

- **Goal:** let people hold notes in the company that owns a refurbished home and share its rent, monthly.
- **Product:** one company (SPV) per home; profit-participating notes; 1.5% acquisition + 0.5%/yr administration; monthly distributions; investor vote on sale; ECSPR bulletin board; Raya Chain mirrors the register.
- **Legal basis:** ECSPR investment-based crowdfunding. Open: AIF classification of a per-home SPV; MiFID status of the note.
- **Need:** SPV structure and note instrument with legal sign-off on fund-rules risk; tax treatment; RTB and tenancy operations; secondary-transfer rules consistent with ECSPR.
- **Risks:** AIF classification; illiquidity expectations; operating-cost drift.

## Stage 3 · Tenant to owner (2028+)

- **Goal:** let a tenant buy into the home they live in, step by step, rent falling as their share grows.
- **Product:** staircasing purchases of notes/shares yearly; investors bought out at valuation with a return; every step on Raya Chain.
- **Legal basis:** requires further authorisation — consumer-credit / mortgage-type permissions if lending is involved; affordability checks; possibly a shared-equity framework.
- **Need:** lending permissions or a partner; affordability assessment with human oversight; a tenancy-to-ownership legal template; tax and stamp-duty clarity.
- **Risks:** affordability and arrears; valuation disputes; investor exit expectations vs tenant pace.

## Stage 4 · Internet of Value (not before 2028; depends on EU legislation)

- **Goal:** Raya Chain as the official register of notes; holdings move across chains; payouts in a regulated euro stablecoin; settlement in central bank money.
- **Product:** Raya Chain as a Layer Zero — master record with anchors, compliance rules travelling with the asset, programmable payouts, wholesale CBDC settlement.
- **Legal basis:** reformed DLT Pilot Regime (Commission proposal of 4 Dec 2025 extending it to all MiFID instruments); MiCA for any e-money token; Eurosystem settlement initiatives.
- **Need:** DLT Pilot Regime licence or partner; custodian; regulated euro stablecoin; audited cross-chain controls; legal recognition of the on-chain register.
- **Risks:** regime timing and scope; technology and key-management risk; interoperability without losing the single master record.
